Hit-or-Myth: Happy Songs, Hit Songs?

A team project for DSC 80 (June 2026) with Kitty Ming. We asked whether happier songs are more popular on Spotify. We found no link between valence and popularity, then built a Random Forest model that explains about half the variation in popularity from audio features.
